# Preliminary fast tests, registered 17.10.2026 before their data was fetched

These are exploratory screens meant to produce decision-changing evidence within hours. They are not confirmatory
proof. A pass leads only to a properly pre-registered forward paper test. Nothing here touches pm_lp_v1: its
methodology, panel, collector and reserved days stay unchanged. No orders, no account, no money.

## P1. Polymarket 15-minute Bitcoin "Up or Down": model vs market (historical trade prints)

**Mechanism.** Resolution is "Up" if the Chainlink BTC/USD 60-s TWAP over the 15-minute window is ≥ the price at the
window start (market rules, checked 17.10). With m minutes left, (15 − m)/15 of the TWAP is already known, so a simple
random-walk model can price the outcome. Lee, Lee & Lee 2026 (https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6748186) report that
Polymarket crypto contracts are "systematically underconfident". If prices lag the known partial average, a model
that is more extreme than the market may beat it after fees.

**Data.**
- Markets: `gamma-api …/events?slug=btc-updown-15m-{start}`, every 15-minute start time.
- Trade prints: `data-api …/v2/trades?condition=…`.
- Binance BTCUSDT 1-minute klines as the proxy for Chainlink.

**Periods** (window starts, UTC):
- Exploration: 2026-10-01 00:00 → 2026-10-07 23:45.
- **Confirmation: 2026-10-08 00:00 → 2026-10-14 23:45**, fetched only after the exploration choice is committed in
  `PRELIM_P1_CHOICE.md`.

**Model.** σ = s.d. of 1-minute log returns over the 60 minutes before the decision. A = mean of the Binance 1-minute
closes in the elapsed part of the window. S_t = last close. S₀ = Binance open at the window start.

P(Up) = Φ( ( ((15 − m)·A + m·S_t)/15 − S₀ ) / ( (m/15) · S_t · σ · √(m/3) ) )

**Decision and costs.**
- Decision at m ∈ {10, 5, 2} minutes before the end.
- Market price p = last Up-token trade within 60 s before the decision; Down price = 1 − p. If there is no trade,
  skip.
- Buy Up if P(Up) − p ≥ θ. Buy Down if p − P(Up) ≥ θ. θ ∈ {0.03, 0.05, 0.08, 0.12}.
- Cost per share: price + 1.0 cent (spread allowance) + 0.07·q·(1 − q) (published crypto taker fee rate). Held to
  resolution (payout from `outcomePrices`).
- Net return per $ = (payout − cost)/cost.

**Exploration choice.** The (m, θ) pair with the highest mean net per trade among pairs with ≥ 100 trades. If no
pair has a positive mean, P1 is **rejected** and confirmation is not fetched.

**Data gate D.** The sign of (full-window Binance 1-minute-close TWAP − S₀) must match the actual resolution in
≥ 95 % of exploration markets. Otherwise the proxy is too poor and P1 is INSUFFICIENT.

**Confirmation gates** (fixed m, θ):
- n ≥ 100 trades;
- mean net per trade > 0;
- one-sided 99 % lower bound > 0 (bootstrap over UTC days, 10,000 draws, seed 5);
- mean net > that of "buy the side priced > 0.5" at the same m (baseline).

All must hold → **PASS (preliminary)** → design a forward paper test. Otherwise → reject.

**Competition caveat (stated before data).** These markets are dominated by bots. A pass on trade prints can still
fail on real queue position and latency.

## P2. Polymarket negative-risk events: sum-of-prices arbitrage (live read-only snapshots)

**Mechanism.** In a complete mutually exclusive set (negRisk, not "augmented"), exactly one YES pays $1.
- If Σ best YES asks + Σ taker fees < 1, buying every YES locks in a profit.
- If Σ best YES bids > 1 + fees, the mirror trade (all NO) does.

Evidence against: Yang, Cheng & Zou 2026 (https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6624718) found such episodes rare, seconds
long and tiny.

**Test.** Three snapshots, 30 minutes apart, of all active, non-augmented negRisk events. Fees use the maximum rate
0.07·p(1 − p) per leg (conservative); a leg needs ≥ 5 shares at the best level.
- **Continue** if ≥ 1 opportunity with ≥ $5 locked profit at best-level size appears in ≥ 2 of 3 snapshots.
- Otherwise **reject**.
